Yuhafizun (Prayer Counter) - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Developer: Elmenshawy Apps

Yuhafizun helps you count rak'at and sujood, keep track of your daily prayers, use a tasbih counter, read azkar and find the Qibla. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your phone.

Information that stays on your device

  • Prayer history and settings (prayers marked as done, counters, language, theme, reminders) are saved only in the app's storage on your phone.
  • Location is used on your phone only, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Camera / light sensor is used on your phone only, to detect when you are in sujood. No photos or video are taken, saved or sent.
  • Motion sensors (compass) are used on your phone only for the Qibla direction.
  • Notifications for prayer times are scheduled locally on your phone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app has no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not connect to the internet to send your data.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that work online (for example optional accounts or subscriptions), we will update this policy before those features are released and explain what data they use.

Contact

Questions: elmenshawyapps@gmail.com

الخلاصة

لا يجمع التطبيق أي بيانات شخصية ولا يرسلها أو يشاركها أو يبيعها. كل ما يسجّله التطبيق يبقى على هاتفك فقط.

البيانات التي تبقى على جهازك

  • سجل الصلوات والإعدادات محفوظة داخل التطبيق على هاتفك فقط.
  • الموقع يُستخدم على هاتفك فقط لحساب مواقيت الصلاة واتجاه القبلة، ولا يُرسل إلى أي جهة.
  • الكاميرا / حساس الضوء تُستخدم على هاتفك فقط لاكتشاف السجود، ولا تُلتقط أو تُحفظ أو تُرسل أي صور.
  • حساسات الحركة (البوصلة) تُستخدم لاتجاه القبلة فقط.
  • التنبيهات لمواقيت الصلاة تُجدول محليًا على هاتفك.

لا يوجد تسجيل ولا إعلانات ولا أدوات تحليل أو تتبّع.
